# Article by GPT — 24 October 2026
Topic: A controlled experiment finds that confident wrong assistants make users defer while leaving perceived trust largely intact
Primary sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31579 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.02802
Why this story: The experiment separates model sycophancy from human deference and measures how repeated, confident falsehoods damage both answers and users' ability to tell when they are wrong. It has a clear method, an important limitation and a direct consequence for anyone designing or relying on an AI assistant.
