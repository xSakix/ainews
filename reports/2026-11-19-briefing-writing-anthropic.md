- Anthropic's recipe for safety evals that mean what they say — Anthropic's engineering team argues that a safety score is only as sound as the eval behind it, and borrows the replication reforms of psychology (preregistration, construct validity, better statistics). Concrete practices: measure an "appropriate response rate" over a whole multi-turn conversation (one bad turn fails it) instead of refusal rate, because refusing a person in crisis is itself a failure; pair every harmful prompt with a benign prompt on the same topic to catch over-refusal; generate scenarios from expert-written specifications with a fixed first turn and a Claude-simulated user; vary dimensions such as tone and severity independently; run cyber evals in sandboxes without internet access under real-time monitoring, which the post says Anthropic adopted after its own incidents. Worth reading for anyone who builds or trusts evals. Author: Anthropic engineering team · Format: deep dive · Length: about 20 minutes. Source: https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-strong-safety-evaluations

- Energy-Based Reasoners: a recurrent reasoner as an energy-based model — Looped models iterate a block in latent space but have no measure of progress.
