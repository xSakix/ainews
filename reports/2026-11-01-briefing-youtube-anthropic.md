## AI engineering and research

- When Running Your Own AI Models Beats Paying for APIs — Steven Latré, head of AI at imec (the Belgian semiconductor R&D centre), reports a benchmarking study of what moving long-running coding agents from paid APIs to your own GPUs really costs. His team ran thousands of runs of open-weight models in three coding harnesses on SWE-Bench Pro. Findings as stated in the description: hardware cost rises in steps, not smoothly; concurrency matters more than accuracy; break-even GPU utilisation runs from 89% on small systems to about 15% on 8x B200 machines; and the choice of harness can halve GPU time for the same result. Useful for anyone deciding between a bill from an API provider and owning hardware.
