## 1. United States

- Cloudflare Clef-omni, plus a faster Clef and a cheaper Clef-flash — Material new development on the Clef decision-model family first covered on 2 October. Clef-omni is a 30B-A3B mixture-of-experts post-trained from Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct (vision and audio encoders kept, speech-output talker and code2wav unused). It reads text, images, audio (wav, mp3) and video (mp4, webm with synchronised soundtrack) and returns a calibrated probability for every option of every typed question in one forward pass, with no generated text. Cloudflare says the Qwen backbone is frozen and trained with LoRA adapters, using label-smoothed cross-entropy plus Brier-score calibration, with a joint schema head that routes evidence from the input to each question. Its own numbers: text-only decisions about 130 ms median, images about 150 ms, a 21-second video with sound about 1.5 s on Workers AI; on its benchmark table Clef-omni trails the dense Clef on most items (for example PhishNChips 73.2 vs 79.6, Home appliances 69.3 vs 82.95) and is ahead on BANKING77 (94.8 vs 94.2). Price changes in the same post: Clef-flash input falls from $0.09 to $0.038 per million tokens, but the hosted Clef-flash context window is cut from 64k to 24k; Clef stays at $0.24 and Clef-omni launches at $0.15. All numbers are vendor-reported. The weights follow the Jev/SystemOne request format; the card lists about 64 GB of GPU memory in bf16 on one H200 and says SGLang support is "coming soon".
