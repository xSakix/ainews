## Safety and security

- CircumVolve: about $1 of GPT-5.1 tokens finds new ways around the Great Firewall — Researchers from UMass Amherst, the University of Colorado Boulder and the activist group Great Firewall Report present "CircumVolve" at ACM CCS. It pairs LLM code generation with a feedback loop: the model writes executable Python that manipulates packets and protocols (examples given: TCP/IP header fields, DNS queries exploiting parser ambiguities, the internal structure of QUIC Initial packets) and tests it against simulated censors modelled on China, Iran, Pakistan and Kazakhstan, run from cloud servers in those countries. Median cost to a first working evasion was under $1 in tokens, with about 80 KB out and 150 KB in per test and 25-45 s pauses to stay unobtrusive. Limits: the censors were simulated, not live national systems; the claim that small payloads and pauses avoid detection is the authors' expectation, not a demonstrated result; The Register does not say how disclosure was handled. The same automation could in principle serve the other side (censors probing for evasion), which is the dual-use tension. Source: https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/23/1-of-gpt-51-tokens-finds-new-ways-around-chinas-great-firewall/5311532
