## Science and philosophy

Michael Tomasello on Machine Learning Street Talk, "What a Squirrel Knows That Language Models Skip" — Tomasello, a developmental psychologist (Duke; twenty years at the Max Planck Institute comparing children and great apes) discusses his book The Evolution of Agency. His argument: where the environment is predictable nature builds behaviour in, where it is not nature builds a decision-maker; he runs this through a traffic light, a thermostat and a robot vacuum, then reptiles, a squirrel that imagines falling and takes another branch, and apes that seek information before committing. The second half covers shared agency and joint goals in humans and what that implies for machines. Directly on the cognitive-science side of the profile, though the application to language models is the interviewer's and speaker's argument, not a test.
