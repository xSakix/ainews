## 1. Safety and security

- **Meta's Muse agent exposed users' private and business content through its share links; Meta has paused sharing** — Ars Technica reports that researchers at Claroc enumerated 6,800 shared-conversation links with "one query" (the link addresses can be found through passive DNS records) and, after opening a few hundred, found a confidential executive-search call prep, a company revenue review marked for its CEO, a loan application, a bank fraud alert with a partial account number and a staff vacation calendar. Claroc's CTO says he reported it through Meta's bug bounty on 8 October and got no reply; Meta told Ars it has "paused this functionality" while it reviews the report. Matters because Zuckerberg called Muse "built from the ground up for privacy and security", and because share-by-link features in agent products are the same pattern behind earlier leaks; the figures are the researchers' own. Source: https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/meta-muse-exposes-users-personal-and-business-secrets-when-they-share-conversations/

- **curl moves low and medium-severity vulnerability reports into public view from 26 October because of an "avalanche" of reports, many AI-assisted** — The Register quotes maintainer Daniel Stenberg: over 90% of submissions in recent years were quickly judged below "high", only high and critical cases stay private, and the team is "brought to the brink of what we can muster". He says researchers using AI generate unusual option combinations unlikely to occur in real use and treat minor discrepancies as flaws; curl already closed its bug bounty this year for the same reason. The trade-off is stated openly: public handling gives attackers more time on unfixed bugs, but spreads the load. Shows the cost of AI-driven bug hunting landing on the maintainers of widely used open-source software. Source: https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/20/swamped-curl-maintainers-take-low-level-flaws-public-to-survive-vulnerability-avalanche/5304198

Shows which parts of the export regime actually bind: demand has moved to the card the rules do not cover. The cluster is to reach about a gigawatt and a million accelerators, with Vera Rubin to follow. Figures are Meta's own; useful as a rare number on what reliability costs at this scale.
