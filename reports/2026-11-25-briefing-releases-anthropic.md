- GPT-6 family shows protein-design ability without specialist biology models (OpenAI, 24 Nov) — OpenAI reports that its GPT-6 models design new protein shapes and sequences through general reasoning and code, described in a preprint. In wet-lab tests 35 of 322 minibinder designs (10.9%) bound their target across five targets, and 128 of 182 (70%) antibody loop redesigns bound IL-4Rα. On PoseBusters docking GPT-6.2 matched the experimental pose in 93% of predictions against 50% for DiffDock-L. Limits stated by OpenAI: specialised structure models still filtered candidates (so not end-to-end), structure prediction is weak (TM-score 0.38 against 0.93 for ESMFold2), some benchmark scores may reflect memorisation, and only binding was tested, not stability, specificity or safety. The results come from an internal research model with no stated access path; all figures are OpenAI's own. - Ternary Bonsai 2.5 27B (Prism ML, 24 Nov) — A ternary-weight (−1, 0, +1; FP16 scale per 128 weights) compression of Qwen3.8-27B at 1.76 bits per weight, 5.93 GB on disk against 53.8 GB for FP16, built for llama.cpp (CUDA, Metal, CPU). Prism reports agentic tool-calling average 77.2 against 77.7 for FP16, SWE-bench Verified 75.6 against 80.6, and 82.1 against 84.3 on a 16-benchmark general average; MTP variants add a draft head claimed to speed decoding 1.5 to 1.8 times on NVIDIA GPUs. Benchmarks are Prism's own. The Bonsai line has been covered before; this is the 2.5 version on a newer base. Source: https://huggingface.co/prism-ml/Ternary-Bonsai-2.5-27B-GGUF
  Artefacts: weights (Apache 2.0), GGUF · Sizes or variants: 27B, PTQ1_0 5.93 GB, Q2_0 7.49 GB, MTP variants, MLX 2-bit · Released: 24 Nov 2026

- Aurora2-400M-A20M-QAT (AuroraAI Research, ~22 Nov) — A tiny research MoE (399.6M total, 19.05M active, 2,048 context) compressed to about 0.5 bits per routed-expert weight with LittleBit-2 and distillation, 27.5 MB against 799 MB.
