## 2. Europe

- LightOnOCR-3 (LightOn, France) — A new family of small document-OCR vision-language models that adds a "grounding" prompt mode on top of plain transcription: every block comes back with a label and a bounding box (coordinates normalised 0–1000), images get short descriptions and charts are converted to tables of their data points. The 1B keeps the LightOnOCR-2 architecture (drop-in upgrade); the 0.8B and 4B move to the Qwen3.5 vision-language architecture for speed. For readers running local document pipelines this replaces a multi-stage layout, OCR and chart-extraction stack with one small model. The blog describes the SFT mixture, an iterative bounding-box data pipeline and RL with checkpoint averaging; benchmark numbers are LightOn's own. Artefacts: weights (Apache 2.0) / technical write-up (blog; arXiv 2601.14251 is the earlier LightOnOCR-2 paper) / code (benchmark reproduction and vLLM client on GitHub) · Sizes or variants: 0.8B, 1B, 4B · Released: 6–7 October 2026 on Hugging Face (4B 6 Oct 16:12 UTC, 1B 7 Oct 09:25, 0.8B 7 Oct 10:23); blog published 8 October 2026. Source: https://huggingface.co/blog/lightonai/lightonocr-3
Model card: https://huggingface.co/lightonai/LightOnOCR-3-4B
