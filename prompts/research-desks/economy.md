# Desk: Economy and work

Desk name for the header: `Economy and work`
Output file: `reports/YYYY-MM-DD-briefing-economy-<suffix>.md`, where `<suffix>` is your system's suffix (see `prompts/research-run.md`)

## Scope

What AI does to work, money and industries: the Consequence lens in `prompts/editorial-profile.md` (tier 1, items 3 and 4).

- **Jobs, skills and professions**: labour-market studies and official statistics, hiring and layoffs explicitly tied to AI, changes in what skills employers ask for, above all in the software profession; what happens to competence when people delegate to agents.
- **How teams work with agents**: companies' own accounts of how they ship, staff, review and manage with AI (engineering blogs, interviews, internal-process write-ups), with specifics.
- **What AI costs**: price and billing changes of AI tools and APIs (subscriptions moved to tokens or credits, plans withdrawn, rate limits), with the before and after.
- **Winners and losers**: market moves with a cause named by the source (a sell-off after a release, a product category displaced), AI-native companies' growth with their own figures and what they sell, infrastructure spending at a scale that moves the market.
- **Frontier capability and access**: system cards, safety incidents during testing, restricted or staged access to frontier models, and the argument over who they are built for.
- **AI in the physical economy**: robots and autonomous systems deployed in factories, warehouses and logistics, with numbers on what is built and what works; national plans that fund it.

Out of scope here: plain funding rounds, valuations and executive moves (the industry desk's "Business, briefly"), policy and court rulings (industry desk), and model releases as such (releases desk). When a release is mainly news because of its consequence (a market reaction, a locked-away model), report the consequence here and let the releases desk report the model.

## Window

48 hours for events (a repricing, a market move, a layoff, an announcement). 7 days for studies, statistics, reports and long write-ups, which spread slowly. Not in any earlier briefing or post, unless there is a material new development.

## Where to look

- Labour and economy data with a published method: national statistics offices (US Bureau of Labor Statistics, Eurostat, the Slovak Statistical Office), central banks, the OECD, the ILO, the IMF, the World Bank; academic working papers (NBER, SSRN, arXiv econ and cs.CY); Stanford HAI, Epoch AI, the Anthropic Economic Index, Microsoft's and LinkedIn's Economic Graph research; job-posting data (Indeed Hiring Lab).
- Pricing and billing: the changelogs and pricing pages of AI coding tools and APIs (GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Windsurf and others), their official announcements, and Hacker News and Reddit threads where users show their bills.
- Companies' own accounts of working with agents: engineering blogs, earnings calls and shareholder letters (the passages about AI), layoff and hiring announcements that name AI.
- Markets: tier-1 outlets for market moves and their causes (Reuters, Bloomberg, Financial Times, The Wall Street Journal, The Information, CNBC), filings.
- Frontier access: labs' system cards, safety and policy posts, model access pages.
- Physical AI: company announcements with deployment figures, industry associations (IFR), national plans, and tier-1 reporting (Reuters, Nikkei Asia, the South China Morning Post, Caixin).

## What to report

For each item, the summary states what changed and for whom, with the number that measures it. Then add one line:
`Who is affected: [group, e.g. developers on Copilot Business, junior engineers in the US, software vendors] · Evidence: [study / statistic / price sheet / filing / company account / tier-1 report / opinion] · Tension: [the gap the sources show, in a few words, or "none"]`

A company's figures are the company's claims; an analyst's estimate is an estimate; an opinion piece is an opinion. Say so. Fill in Tension only with a gap the sources themselves show.
