## 1. United States

- Mass Spec Skills for Google Antigravity (Google DeepMind, 25 Nov) — Google ships a set of domain skills that let a Gemini-driven agent interpret tandem mass spectra, from peak cleanup to ranking candidate structures from PubChem. On a 100-compound benchmark Google reports under 4% accuracy with no internet or skills and about 97% with internet plus the skills, across all three Gemini models tested; tokens per attempt fell nearly 4x on Gemini 3.8 Flash. Caveats stated by Google: library search annotates only about 5% of measured spectra (10% in human samples), the benchmark used compounds that already have public reference spectra, attempts over one hour scored as failures, and mirror-image or close isomers need other measurements. The highest Antigravity usage tier is free for six months for 10,000 scientists (rolling, subject to selection). Artefacts: API/product only (Antigravity plugin, no weights) · Sizes or variants: skills for Gemini 3.8 Flash, 4 Argon, 4 Carbon · Released: 25 Nov 2026. Source: https://deepmind.google/blog/interpreting-mass-spectra-with-google-antigravity/

- Muse comes to Instagram DMs (Meta, 24 Nov) — Meta's Muse agent can now be called inside Instagram DM threads (ask about a post, summarise missed DMs, add a place to an itinerary) and prompts to organise saved posts into collections. The DM integration rolls out on iOS and Android in the US, Canada, Mexico, Japan and Singapore; the save-organising prompt only in the US and Canada. TechCrunch gives no usage figures and does not name the underlying model. Artefacts: product only · Sizes or variants: n/a · Released: 24 Nov 2026. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/24/meta-brings-its-muse-ai-assistant-directly-into-instagram-dms/

- Cosmos 3 Nano on consumer GPUs (NVIDIA, 24 Nov) — NVIDIA's technical blog shows its Cosmos 3 Nano video world model running through Hugging Face Diffusers with an offloading adapter on a 12 GB card. NVIDIA's own single-run timings for 720p text-to-video in BF16: RTX 5070 (12 GB) 2,625 s at 9.9 GiB peak (FP8: 2,236 s), RTX 4090 1,052 s, RTX 5090 1,017 s; 240p on the 5070 takes 208 s. The test hosts had about 128 GB system RAM, which the post says is not a measured minimum. The post gives no parameter count or licence (points to the model card for `nvidia/Cosmos3-Nano`). Artefacts: weights (licence not stated in post) / code · Sizes or variants: Nano, BF16 and native FP8 · Released: 24 Nov 2026 (blog). Source: https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/how-to-run-nvidia-cosmos-3-on-local-ai-devices-using-hugging-face-diffusers/

- Astra used by amateurs to search Kepler/TESS data (OpenAI, 24 Nov) — OpenAI's account of two amateur astronomers using Astra and Codex to write search code over public data: one searched about 100,000 Kepler stars and flagged more than 200 signals (10 from already confirmed planets); the other searched about 19 million stars and produced 49 first-pass candidates, one sent to the TESS team. Nothing is confirmed (months to years), eclipsing binaries can mimic dips, and the post releases no code or candidate list. A product-use story rather than a model release; useful as a measured example of agentic tooling in a science workflow. Artefacts: none (product use case) · Sizes or variants: n/a · Released: 24 Nov 2026. Source: https://openai.com/index/searching-for-distant-planets-with-astra/

- Hill-climbing Inkling 2 Mini inference on one RTX PRO 6000 (Hugging Face, 25 Nov) — HF tuned serving of Thinking Machines' Inkling 2 Mini (looped MoE, about 26B total / 3B active, 1M context) using a coding agent over an afternoon of HF Jobs. Single-user decode rose from 136 to 190.6 tok/s (+40%) and 8-user aggregate from about 570 to 716 tok/s (+26%), about 95% of the card's bandwidth ceiling (roughly 201 tok/s with speculation). Gains came from moving SGLang to vLLM nightly (about +15%) and DSpark speculative decoding with 7 drafts (about +21%). Limits: six prompts, 512 output tokens, temperature 1.0, published recipes target B200/GB300 not this card's sm120, and SGLang run-to-run variance was about 4.5% against 0.4% for vLLM. Artefacts: technical write-up · Released: 25 Nov 2026. Source: https://huggingface.co/blog/inkling-2-mini-inference-hillclimb
