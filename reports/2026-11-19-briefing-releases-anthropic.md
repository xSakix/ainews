## United States

- 🚨 Google ships Gemini 4 Neon, a mid-tier model priced at $3 / $15 per million tokens — Google positions Neon between Gemini 4 Carbon (hardest long-horizon work) and Gemini 4 Hydrogen (fast everyday use). It targets software engineering, knowledge work and science, with four thinking levels and a 1M-token context window. Google says cost per task at the default "high" level is 32% below Carbon's and roughly 50–80% below top competing frontier models on the Vals and Agent Arena frontiers. It reports Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index 61 (#3 overall at xhigh), Vals Index 70.9% (#2), 65.2% on Terminal-Bench 4.1, 46.2% on FrontierCode 2.0 and 71.3% on HLE-Diamond; all of these are the lab's own claims, several quoted from third-party leaderboards, and the post is promotional. The safety section reports a 0.09% misaligned-action rate, 99.2% agent-honesty pass rate and a 0.0% indirect prompt-injection success rate at the highest thinking level; the model card is linked from the post. A cyber-focused variant goes only to vetted defenders in the "Fairwind Program" over the coming days, which makes this also a staged-access story. Artefacts: API only (Gemini API paid tier in AI Studio, Android Studio, Antigravity, Gemini for business; Gemini app dropdown for AI Pro and Ultra) · model card · Sizes or variants: four thinking levels, cyber variant for trusted defenders · Released: 18 November 2026. Source: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-neon/

- Genie 4 becomes available inside Project Genie (new development: the model itself was covered earlier, this is first public access) — Google DeepMind offers Genie 4, its most realistic interactive world model, to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers from 18 November; AI Pro members join a waitlist. Users can change weather, add characters or redirect the action by typing prompts mid-session, and the model adds real-time native audio that adapts to the scene. The release also extends Genie 3 sessions from 60 seconds to 3 minutes per generation, adds a Project Genie agent that builds worlds from conversation and can call Gemini to observe the screen, and opens a community gallery for remixing. No Genie 4 session limits or prices are given; outputs carry SynthID watermarks and pass safety screening. Artefacts: product access only (no weights) · Sizes or variants: Genie 4, Genie 3 (upgraded) · Released: 18 November 2026. Source: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/genie-4-now-available-project-genie/

- Microsoft publishes Aion 1.0 Instruct, a 2.3B on-device model that will become the Windows OS small language model — Aion 1.0 Instruct is a 28-layer causal model (~2.29B parameters, hybrid layout of 21 sliding-window and 7 full-attention layers, 32K context) covering English, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, French, Chinese and Japanese, built by the Windows Applied Sciences Group for summarisation, rewriting and chat. Microsoft says a further optimised version, paired with its content-moderation model, becomes the Windows OS SLM by the end of January 2027; the Hugging Face release is an early, transparent preview, with quantised builds to follow (an ONNX WebGPU repo is already up). Self-reported scores are modest by design (MMLU 55.2, MMLU-Pro 33.4, GSM8K 74.9, IFEval 83.9, GPQA 28.2, GPT-4o as judge where one was needed). It matters because it is the model that will run inside Windows itself, openly licensed under MIT. Artefacts: weights (MIT), model card, sample code on GitHub · Sizes or variants: 2.3B instruct; ONNX WebGPU variant · Released: 18 November 2026. Source: https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Aion-1.0-Instruct-2.3B

- Google Labs opens a waitlist for Stitch Loop, an agent that finds product problems and opens pull requests — Stitch Loop connects to a codebase (GitHub) and live product data (logs, user behaviour, feedback via 180+ integrations, custom MCP servers or CLI uploads), then runs a four-step cycle: surface evidence-backed suggestions, offer fixes with trade-offs, build the chosen one in an isolated cloud sandbox and open a PR, and monitor live metrics afterwards. Default loops cover cybersecurity (using Gemini 4 Carbon and DeepMind's CodeMender), latency and accessibility; nothing merges without human review. Waitlist is global, with priority for AI Ultra subscribers. Artefacts: product waitlist, no weights · Released: 18 November 2026. Source: https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/stitch-loop/

## China

- Tencent releases Hy4 flash, an open 220B MoE with 12B active parameters and a 1M-token context, plus FP8 and base checkpoints — Hy4 flash (155.5B parameters excluding 63.5B "Engram" n-gram embedding parameters, 0.43B vision encoder, 0.85B built-in DFly2 speculative-decoding drafter) takes text and images and generates text. It interleaves sparse and sliding-window attention 1:3, routes 16 of 512 experts per token and, Tencent says, cuts KV cache to 1/24 of Hy3 and 1/6 of Hy4. Tencent's own claims: 42.1 on its internal WorkIndex (Hy4 preview 39.7, Hy4 51.4) at 1/13 the cost per task of Hy4 preview and 1/8 of DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017; user satisfaction of 2.79/4 against Claude Haiku 5.5's 2.81/4 in a blind test of 85 internal users over 259 tasks, at RMB 0.26 per task; decoding above 150 tokens/s. These are vendor figures on an internal benchmark and an internal user panel, not independent measurements. Tencent recommends it for deep search, documents, slides and computer use, and the larger Hy4 for long-horizon software engineering. Community quantisations (GGUF, EXL3) appeared within a day. Artefacts: weights (Tencent Hy License, not an OSI licence), base model, FP8, technical write-up on the model card, GitHub code · Sizes or variants: Hy4 flash, Hy4 flash-FP8, Hy4 flash-base · Released: 17 November 2026. Source: https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy4-flash
