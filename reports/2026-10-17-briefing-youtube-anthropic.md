- Rebuilding CI for AI-Scale Commit Volume — Gautam Korlam (Sonar, formerly Uber SubmitQueue and Gitar) argues that coding agents produce changes faster than the merge queues, builds and reviews built for human arrival rates can absorb, so the bottleneck moves to failed builds, repeated repairs and approvals. His remedies: incremental builds, caching and sharding, durable failure context so repair loops stop rediscovering the same problem, risk-matched approval policies that separate authorship from approval and bind the decision to the exact tested commit. Volume and latency figures are examples from his own systems, not general productivity guarantees. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7b9tov5Uc5s
