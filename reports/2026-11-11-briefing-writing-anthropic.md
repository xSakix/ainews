- Optimizing Meta's 816B Muse Spark Open on one four-chip TPU v7 host with SGLang and a decode "megakernel" — The RadixArk team runs all 62 layers of a decode or verify step as one Pallas program with every weight load, KV read and cross-device transfer pipelined behind compute. ## Essays and arguments

- What objective is reasoning solving? An essay that frames chain-of-thought as pathfinding in token space — Alex Wang argues that RL trains models to raise the probability that some sampled path reaches a verified answer, not that each chain converges on the truth. That would explain why forcing one chain to run longer helps then stops helping (and sometimes hurts), why chains of thought need not be faithful, and why reward hacking can come from both the verifier and the sampler; he works through best-of-k sampling and amortisation. It is the author's own framing ("mostly for myself"), a derivation and not an experiment, so read it as a model of reasoning, not an established finding. Posted 8 Nov 2026, on the Hacker News front page. Author: Alex Wang, independent researcher · Format: essay (with maths) · Length: about 20 min. Source: https://alexwang.ai/posts/reasoning-as-pathfinding/
