- How to Actually Tokenmaxx and Save Millions — Alvin Pane (OutRival) describes a workflow for cutting LLM cost without silently losing quality: log prompt versions, models, tokens, latency and cost first; use prefix caching; shorten instructions with DSPy and GEPA (a 51% prompt reduction in his synthetic example); and test a cheaper candidate against production by shadow evaluation.
