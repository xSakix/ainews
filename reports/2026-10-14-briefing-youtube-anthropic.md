## AI engineering and research

David Silver and Lasse Espeholt (Ineffable Intelligence), "A Superlearner for the Age of Experience" — Silver, who led AlphaGo and AlphaGo Zero at DeepMind, and Espeholt, who built the IMPALA and SEED RL lineage of large-scale RL, describe their new lab's plan: an agent that starts with no human data and discovers science, mathematics and language through direct experience instead of a pre-training run. Silver argues that human-language reasoning is a ceiling rather than a foundation, that chain-of-thought monitoring as an alignment method is already reaching its limits, and that coexistence of superintelligences should be studied in simulation first; Espeholt covers the GPU cluster and RL stack. These are the founders' own claims and forecasts (Silver predicts strong evidence within a year and superintelligence in three), not results. Hosted by Sonya Huang of Sequoia.
