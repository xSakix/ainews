# {{ site.Title }}

> {{ site.Params.description }}

AI News Daily publishes up to six articles and one digest every morning (Europe/Bratislava time). Each article ends with a verification table in which every checkable claim carries one label (VERIFIED, VENDOR-REPORTED, PARTIALLY VERIFIED or UNVERIFIED) and a link to its primary source. Articles are researched and drafted with AI models under the editorial rules described on the About page.

Every article is available as Markdown: append `index.md` to its URL. The full text of the most recent articles is in [llms-full.txt]({{ "llms-full.txt" | absURL }}).
