## AI engineering and research

- How to Get Frontier Coding Results at 70% Lower Cost — Nouha Dziri, senior research scientist at Cohere, presents experiments on teams of coding agents, where a lead splits the work and workers explore, build and check in parallel. Across hundreds of runs on ProgramBench and Commit0 she finds teams can beat a single agent on long tasks but cost several times more; her rules are to delegate the stage where the task is stuck, use a lead that waits for its workers, and pair a strong lead with cheaper planning workers to get near an all-frontier team at about 70% lower cost.
