## Safety and security

- Cursor links its marketplace and data incident to a Fireworks AI key — Cursor says an attacker used an access key it had issued to inference provider Fireworks AI, which Fireworks exposed in its own breach, to copy a read-only storage bucket of coding-agent traces from sessions outside Privacy Mode (mostly 2025, some containing unredacted credentials; under 0.01% of customers' API credentials). Separately, on 29 October a stolen internal credential was used to place trojanized Claude Code extension builds in the cache behind Cursor's marketplace for about 11 hours (up to 5,000 users; affected versions 2.1.312 and 2.1.313). Cursor has rotated credentials, moved inference off Fireworks and taken the marketplace offline, and reports no impact on Privacy Mode users or customer repositories. The Fireworks link is the new development; it ties a supply-chain incident in an agent tool to a third-party inference vendor whose breach also reportedly reached other customers. Source: https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/stolen-fireworks-ai-key-led-to-cursor.html (first disclosure of the extension incident, 14 November: https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cursor-confirms-security-breach-led-to-malicious-claude-code-extension/)

- 🚨 FBI warns that the open-source AI pentest framework ARTEX is used in real intrusions — A FLASH advisory (released Friday 13 November, reported 15 November) says several threat actors have used ARTEX since at least August 2026 against finance, government, technology, defense and healthcare targets, with some intrusions reaching privileged access in under three hours. ARTEX is a Go framework that drives reconnaissance, exploitation and data theft through external LLMs (Claude, GPT, DeepSeek) rather than its own model. Its original developer pulled the repository in October, but forks and a commercial edition remain; researchers counted more than 1,600 exposed ARTEX consoles in early November. The tension is between agentic offensive tooling that lowers the skill needed and the defenders' response, which is still rate-limiting, logging and detection signatures (an open-source signature pack exists).
