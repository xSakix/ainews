- OpenBMB releases Trident-Qwen3VL-2B, an MIT-licensed multimodal embedding model aimed at a bias it found in mixed-modality retrievers — The accompanying paper, "Chaos in the Text", finds that retrievers rank text representations higher than equally relevant images, so irrelevant text can outrank relevant images when text, images and fused text-image documents share one corpus. The fix is to build text, image and fused views of each document as co-equal positives and train with a Multi-Positive View InfoNCE loss. The model is Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct fine-tuned with a merged LoRA adapter and maps text, image and fused inputs into one space. The result is useful for anyone building multimodal retrieval over mixed document stores. The gains are the authors' own. Artefacts: weights (MIT), training data Trident-Train, evaluation data Trident-Eval, code (github.com/OpenBMB/Trident), paper (arXiv 2610.11816, v1 8 October) · Sizes or variants: 2B only · Released: model page created 12 October 2026 (paper 8 October). ## 5. Tools, APIs and platform updates

- NVIDIA and Princeton's Dao AI Lab publish ReplaySSM, a way to make state-space layers fast again in hybrid models — Hybrid models such as Nemotron 3, Qwen3.5 to 3.8, Kimi-K3 and GLM-5.3-Flash interleave state-space layers (Mamba-2, Gated DeltaNet, Kimi Delta Attention) with a few attention layers, and at serving batch sizes the state layers can become the decoding bottleneck. ReplaySSM stores the state as a checkpoint plus a buffer of recent inputs. Most steps then skip state writes (roughly half the state memory traffic), speculative-decoding rollback becomes a buffer-pointer move, and a low-precision copy can serve reads while a high-precision checkpoint handles updates. NVIDIA's blog reports 1.46x to 1.80x faster decoding kernels at batch 256 on Blackwell Ultra B300 and 1.85x to 2.10x higher speculative throughput on 120B-class models. The paper's end-to-end numbers in vLLM are smaller: 1.20x to 1.47x over a tuned baseline, about 1.8x speculative throughput. These are the authors' own measurements. Artefacts: code (GitHub, linked from the blog), paper (arXiv 2610.13778, v1 9 October), TensorRT-LLM support · Sizes or variants: implemented for Mamba-2 and Gated DeltaNet in vLLM · Released: blog 12 October 2026 (paper 9 October). Source: https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/replayssm-rethinking-state-storage-for-faster-hybrid-model-decoding/
