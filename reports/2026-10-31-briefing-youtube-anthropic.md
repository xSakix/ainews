- Language models recognize and inhibit interventions to their activations — A Mila seminar (the description is the paper abstract; the speaker is not named) reports that language models can detect, localize and verbalize what kind of perturbation was applied to their own activations (dropout, Gaussian noise or steering vectors), and can often learn in context to classify the perturbation's size and layer.
