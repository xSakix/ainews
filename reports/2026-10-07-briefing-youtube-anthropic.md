## AI engineering and research

Stefan Rabanser (Princeton), Adam Gleave (FAR.AI) and Dylan Hadfield-Menell (MIT), panel at the Alignment Workshop — "The Capability-Reliability Gap: Why AI Agents Still Fail in the Real World" — Rabanser proposes four ways to measure agents the way aviation or nuclear engineering would: consistency on repeated identical inputs, robustness under perturbation, predictability (does the model know when it does not know) and bounded severity of failure; his data shows accuracy rising across model generations while reliability stays comparatively flat. Gleave argues reliability is hard to measure and has no scaling-law recipe, and favours an engineering approach with white-box evaluation over interpretability or provable safety. Hadfield-Menell blames training that overfits verifiable proxy metrics. Close to the calibration and self-knowledge theme; the panel's claims are their own.
