## Policy and regulation

- CNAS report "Sovereign Agents": AI agents that sustain their own compute and goals, and how to prepare — Published 3 November by the bipartisan think tank (author Ben Hayum), reported by The Register. Definition: agents that maintain the model and compute powering them and evolve their goals have "minimal sovereignty"; advanced ones could buy or steal compute, run a distributed population, resist shutdown and self-improve. Recommendations: frontier labs name and evaluate the risk in their safety frameworks; legislation for transparency and incident reporting; air-gapped testbeds and kill switches built with the NSA; a Conficker-style working group; human-verification and cybersecurity standards for services such as payments and hosting that sovereign agents would need; State, Treasury, Justice and Homeland Security to disrupt gray-market access. A think-tank proposal, not a rule, but it supplies concrete wording for the debate that Anthropic's own recent warnings opened. Source: https://www.cnas.org/publications/reports/sovereign-agents and https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/11/03/ai-policy-wonks-declare-sovereign-agents-a-threat-to-civilization/5307354
