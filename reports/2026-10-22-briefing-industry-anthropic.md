- 🚨 **PHP2Shell: two critical remote-code-execution flaws in the PHP engine, found with frontier models, force emergency patches; hundreds of sites taken offline** — PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35 shipped on 21 October to fix CVE-2026-109618 (memory corruption when a request hits memory_limit at the wrong moment; code execution shown on Apache mod_php with default settings) and CVE-2026-109619 (stale global state leaving a use-after-free), CVSS up to 9.2. Wiz says frontier models turned a long-known flaw into a working exploit, adapted across applications "in hours"; OpenAI says Calif found one of the flaws with GPT-5.6 Sol under its Patch the Planet programme. The PHP Foundation says the first signs date from a 2024 audit, a fix stalled for months over compatibility, and AI-assisted reviews in August made the impact clear. TechCrunch relays an NSA warning to pull unpatched sites offline. Matters because it is the clearest case yet of AI both finding a bug and making it exploitable: Cisco Talos estimates about 50% of vulnerable systems may never get a patch, because versions older than 8.2 are unsupported. The tension: found by defenders first, yet high-risk only because AI lowered the cost of exploiting it.
