- AI Writes the Code. So What's Left for Engineers? — Laurie Voss, head of developer relations at Arize AI and co-founder of npm, argues that coding agents made writing code nearly free, so the bottleneck keeps moving — first to code review, then to operations and security — while the one thing agents cannot take over is working out what people actually need and defining what "good" means.
