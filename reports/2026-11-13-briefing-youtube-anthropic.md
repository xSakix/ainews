Interpretability, indirection and introspection (David Bau, Northeastern University) — Bau, who directs the National Deep Inference Fabric and created ROME and Network Dissection, asks whether an LLM's words "I know" or "I think" describe anything inside it. He describes a simple procedure that implants new knowledge into a model so that introspection can be measured, then uses it to localise introspection in the network's internal states and relate it to other task performance, model size, metalearning and training. The stated question is whether introspection can be grounded in causal neural states, with implications for AI safety and auditing. A direct fit for the cognitive-side interest: metacognition and self-knowledge in models, with mechanisms rather than behaviour alone.
