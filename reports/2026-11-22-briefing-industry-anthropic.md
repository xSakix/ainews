## 1. Safety and security

- Dragos test finds AI intermediaries leak prompt secrets — Security researcher HD Moore (Dragos) sent nearly two million prompts, each carrying a unique fake secret, through AI hosting providers and resellers. At least three providers were compromised, attackers reused "a couple hundred" of the planted AWS keys, and the keys were then used "tens of thousands" of times in AWS calls; some resellers silently logged prompts and some providers ignored zero-data-retention flags. Prompts are protected in transit but readable at each intermediate hop; practical consequence: keep live keys out of prompts and treat third-party model routers as data processors. Caveat: figures are as summarised in a newsletter digest of the Ars Technica story (Ars could not be fetched directly), and the test design is the researcher's own. Source: https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/a-leaky-ai-supply-chain-is-exposing-secret-user-data-at-an-alarming-rate/
- GAO report on AI and cybersecurity (GAO-27-108458, 20 November) — GAO sets out four policy goals and 16 policy options (guidance gaps, research gaps, lifecycle governance, workforce). It says AI lowers barriers to attacks, with agentic AI that chains attack stages with little human input the main concern, and cites incidents from February 2026 (low-skill actor using commercial generative AI against stolen credentials) and June 2026 (an actor using multiple agents to test malicious code against security products). Defenders gain from AI for traffic analysis and code scanning but face no accepted criteria for evaluating offensive or defensive AI. Caveat: the options are for Congress and agencies to consider, not requirements. Source: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-27-108458
- METR Q4 AI R&D capability update (20 November) — METR reports a task-length doubling time of about 3.61 months (95% CI 2.08–4.94) across its suites, 1.65 months on verifiable tasks only, with frontier models reaching an estimated 27-hour time horizon on that subset; naive extrapolation reaches a 200-hour horizon around July 2027. Models are weaker than expert humans on tasks with slow or unclear feedback (model judges rated model outputs about 0.2 of 1 higher than a human expert did), and METR says public evidence cannot rule out drastic AI R&D acceleration within a couple of years. Caveats: only 9% of human time estimates are directly measured, models may be under-elicited, and verifiable evals may overlap RL training data. Source: https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-20-q4-2026-capability-update/
- Signal restricts screenshots to keep OS assistants out of chats (21 November) — Signal now blocks screenshots on iOS 27 and Android so assistants such as Siri and Gemini cannot read on-screen chats; it cannot offer the same protection on macOS, and president Meredith Whittaker calls the fix incomplete because agents can reach app data in other ways. Users can switch the restriction off, which Whittaker says breaks the privacy guarantee. Caveat: this is a mitigation, not a full defence; TechCrunch (21 November) is the source, with no figures. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/21/signal-restricts-mobile-screenshots-as-it-confronts-existential-threat-of-ai-surveillance/
