## Europe

Quiet at the large labs (Mistral's newest post is 5 Nov). The Russian-language items below are listed under Europe because Russian labs are not covered elsewhere in the desk's regions.

- GigaAM-v4 and GigaAM Multilingual-v2 (Sber's ai-sage), open speech-recognition models — Two MIT-licensed ASR models went up on 26 Nov. GigaAM-v4 (238M parameters RNN-T, 234M CTC) is trained on 7M hours of unlabelled audio plus 58,000 hours of supervised speech, for Russian and English, with streaming at 160/320/640 ms. On the card's own Russian tables it reaches 3.62% WER on Golos Farfield and 6.71% on in-house Callcenter data, against 16.11% and 25.96% for Whisper large-v3; Nvidia's Parakeet Ultra still wins Golos Crowd (2.86% vs 4.56%). Multilingual-v2 (776M parameters) covers 21 languages on 155,000 hours of supervised speech and reports an average WER of 3.96% against 44.09% for Whisper large-v3 (the Whisper figure is inflated by languages such as Bashkir and Kyrgyz where it scores near 100%). All numbers are the lab's own, partly on in-house test sets. Source: https://huggingface.co/ai-sage/GigaAM-v4 and https://huggingface.co/ai-sage/GigaAM-Multilingual-v2
  Artefacts: weights (MIT) · Sizes or variants: v4 RNN-T 238M / CTC 234M / ssl; Multilingual-v2 776M · Released: 26 Nov 2026

- GigaEdge-LLM (Sber's ai-sage), a tiny on-device model for smart-home control — A 129.3M-parameter Russian model (160.1M with four multi-token-prediction heads for speculative decoding) that turns a request plus the home's device list into parallel tool calls for Home Assistant and replies in Russian, running on CPU offline. The shipped Q4_0 GGUF is 90.6 MB; the vocabulary was pruned from 128k to 8,448 tokens and training used about 600k synthetic dialogues and GRPO. The card's score table (1 to 5, LLM judge Gemma 4 31B, six internal Russian benchmarks) lists Gemma 4 E4B at 4.60 and Qwen 3.5 4B at 4.52 as comparators; the benchmarks are internal and the model is deliberately limited to smart-home control (it does not check whether a device is on). Source: https://huggingface.co/ai-sage/GigaEdge-LLM
  Artefacts: weights (MIT, safetensors BF16 plus GGUF) · Sizes or variants: 129.3M AR / 160.1M with MTP · Released: 25 Nov 2026

- RuModernBert (MTS AI), Russian-English encoders — RuModernBertBase (149M parameters, 22 layers, 8,192-token context) was made by swapping mmBERT-base's vocabulary for a 50,368-token BPE one and continuing masked-language-model training for about 88B tokens of Russian and English web text and code; Small and a merged variant were uploaded the same evening. It is an encoder backbone with no task head, and the card gives no benchmark in the part read. Source: https://huggingface.co/MTSAIR/RuModernBertBase
  Artefacts: weights (Apache-2.0) · Sizes or variants: Small, Base, BaseTripleMerge · Released: 25 Nov 2026

## China

- Sage-Med-Preview (Shanghai AI Laboratory, InternLM), a medical multimodal MoE — Built on Intern-S2-Preview with 35B total and 3B active parameters, trained on about 164M records and 226B tokens, with continual pretraining, three-stage SFT and difficulty-adaptive RL. The card reports an average of 58.26 across 31 public medical multimodal benchmarks, against 52.78 for GPT-5.4, 49.18 for Claude-Opus-4.6 and 50.99 for Hulu-Med-235B-A22B (note these comparators are older frontier models), and says it beats GPT-6.2-Sol on a comprehensive understanding score (80.05 vs 76.81) and on MIMIC-CXR report generation (33.37 vs 30.31 ROUGE-L). It also claims first place on nine held-out private tasks from 14 hospitals (57.72 vs 51.24), which outsiders cannot check. All figures are the lab's own; the card does not state a licence in its metadata, and the model is about 131 GiB, tested on one H200 with vLLM. A paper is linked at arxiv.org/abs/2611.34575. Source: https://huggingface.co/internlm/Sage-Med-Preview
  Artefacts: weights (licence not stated in card metadata) / technical report / code · Sizes or variants: 35B-A3B · Released: 25 to 27 Nov 2026 (the HF org copy was created 25 Nov, the internlm copy 27 Nov)

- Innata-0 (BAAI), a world-action model for robot manipulation — BAAI's embodied-AI team released Innata-0-WAM checkpoints (about 16.18B parameters, three-tower Mixture-of-Transformers) for RoboCasa365 and RoboDojo, with inference code and a 40-author report (arXiv v1, 23 Nov). The model takes three camera views, a 16-dimension robot state and an instruction, and predicts 16 actions at 20 Hz; it depends on the separately licensed Wan2.2 VAE. The arXiv abstract gives no numbers, and the card says full closed-loop RoboCasa365 evaluation of the released checkpoint has not been completed, so capability claims are unverified. Source: https://huggingface.co/BAAI/Innata-0-WAM-RoboCasa365 (report: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30254)
  Artefacts: weights (Apache-2.0) / technical report / inference code · Sizes or variants: RoboCasa365, RoboDojo; about 16.18B · Released: 26 Nov 2026
