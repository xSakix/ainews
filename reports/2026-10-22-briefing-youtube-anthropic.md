Does Code Quality Still Matter? It Depends on This — Max Kanat-Alexander of Capital One argues that code quality exists for two reasons, understanding a system and changing it safely. AI is making a new class of disposable software (few dependents, near-zero cost to replace) where quality does not matter, while for durable software it matters more than ever. He then shows how to make systems more disposable and how to make rigor cheap through standards, in-workflow tooling, easy testing and maximum strictness.
