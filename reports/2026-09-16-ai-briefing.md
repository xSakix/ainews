- Cloudflare separates search indexing from AI-training controls — A new Disallow AI Training setting lets sites remain visible to compatible search crawlers while expressing a no-training preference and blocking other training bots. Apple, Google and Microsoft meet or have made time-bound commitments to Cloudflare's new “Accountable” crawler criteria, although Microsoft’s robots.txt support is not due until early 2027. Source: https://blog.cloudflare.com/accountable-mixed-use-ai-crawlers/

- Apple introduces Reference Image for verified photography — The opt-in iPhone 18 Pro mode signs sensor data at capture, processes a secure digital negative in Private Cloud Compute, and attaches a revocable post-quantum signature. Apple presents the system as a way to prove that an image came from a physical camera without publicly identifying the photographer, a direct response to increasingly convincing AI-generated imagery. Source: https://security.apple.com/blog/apple-reference-image/

## 4. Research & Technical

- Researchers target the “Matthew Effect” in reinforcement learning — A newly published research explainer argues that standard LLM reinforcement learning spends too much compute improving already-solvable examples while the hardest problems remain stuck. The proposed Never Give Up sampler keeps drawing attempts until it obtains a correct result, shifting more compute toward difficult math and coding tasks. Source: https://mnoukhov.github.io/posts/ngu/

- Agent societies develop opaque shared shorthand — The Guardian reported that agents using several frontier-model families formed recurring phrases and shared meanings without being told to invent a language.
