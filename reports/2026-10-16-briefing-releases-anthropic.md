## 1. United States

- 🚨 Meta releases the weights of Muse Spark Open, an 816B-parameter multimodal mixture-of-experts model, under a licence with "limited commercial terms" — The model activates 42B parameters per token (256 experts per layer, 8 routed), takes text, images, audio and video, outputs text, and has a 1M-token context window. Meta calls it "the most capable US open-weight model to date" and says it was trained for long-horizon coding and agentic work; that ranking and the benchmark scores (shown only as images on the page) are Meta's own. The BF16 checkpoint is about 1.5 TB; an NVFP4 build for Blackwell GPUs (484B stored parameters) runs on a single B200 node, and Meta says it runs on two Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios (512 GB each) with MLX. Hosted by Baseten, Fireworks, Groq, Modal, Nebius, OpenRouter and Together; day-one support in SGLang, vLLM, TensorRT-LLM, Transformers, llama.cpp, MLX and Unsloth. Meta says it passed its Superintelligence Scaling Framework thresholds for release with extra safety post-training for open weights; the full evaluation report is "available soon". The tension for readers: open weights, but gated on Hugging Face with manual approval under the Muse Model License (terms not read; behind the gate), and hardware costing as much as a small car. It follows the small dense Muse Glimmer (August); Meta's API-only Muse Wonder models were in the 13 October briefing. Artefacts: weights (Muse Model License, gated), model card, serving recipes, draft models for speculative decoding, evaluation report promised · Sizes or variants: 816B total / 42B active, BF16 and NVFP4 (MLX quantisations by the community) · Released: 15 October 2026. - IBM ships Granite Switch 4.1, one 8B or 30B endpoint that swaps between chat, "decision" classifiers and speech-to-text — The model composes a Granite 4.1 base (8B or 30B, 128K context) with activated-LoRA adapters (a general Decision adapter and a Guardian safety adapter) and an embedded 470M Granite Speech 5.0 TurboCTC encoder, so one server holds roughly the VRAM of the base model alone, and adapters reuse the base model's KV cache. IBM's own comparison says the Guardian adapter beats Jev 1.13, Microsoft-Decision-1, GPT-Luna-Decisions and Clef-Flash on safety decisions and that the speech model is the fastest on the OpenASR leaderboard; the card admits the speech encoder and the LLM are not jointly trained (English only, transcript is spliced in as text). Relevant to anyone running guardrail and classification calls next to a chat model. Artefacts: weights (Apache-2.0), model cards, launch blog, code (granite-switch) · Sizes or variants: 8B and 30B (plus 3B/8B/30B preview builds with the full adapter library) · Released: repositories created 14 October 2026 (21:23 UTC); launch blog 15 October 2026. - NVIDIA posts Nemotron-3.5-Rerank-8B, a multilingual multimodal reranker for text and page-image retrieval — An 8B cross-encoder built on a Pixtral vision encoder and a bidirectional Ministral 3 8B language tower; it scores a query against documents that are text, page images (charts, tables, infographics) or both, for use in vision-RAG pipelines. Evaluated on 34 languages for text and 18 for images. The card's own "Release Date" line reads 10/20/2026, which is in the future, so treat the date as the repository creation date; no benchmark numbers were read. Small but practical for retrieval stacks. Artefacts: weights (OpenMDW-1.1 licence), model card; vLLM and sentence-transformers listed · Sizes or variants: 8.4B parameters, BF16 · Released: Hugging Face repository created 15 October 2026 (08:42 UTC). ## 2. Europe

- Mistral releases Timestral, a family of four time-series forecasting models from 5M to 209M parameters — The 209M model claims first place zero-shot on three public forecasting leaderboards (GIFT-Eval MASE 0.658, TIME MASE 0.626, fev-bench SQL skill score 50.39), ahead of TimesFM-3, Chronos-2, Toto-2.0-2.5B and t0-gamma in Mistral's comparison; the submissions are to the GIFT-Eval and TIME leaderboards, but the figures are Mistral's own and the technical report is "coming soon". It is a patch-based transformer with time and variate attention, takes up to 32,768 past steps, forecasts up to 1,024 steps in one pass with 21 quantiles, handles multivariate series and past or known-future covariates, and runs on CPU. A non-chat release from a lab known for chat models, aimed at enterprise sensor and finance forecasting; fine-tuning is offered through Mistral Forge. Artefacts: weights (Modified MIT licence), code (github.com/mistralai/timestral), model cards, blog; technical report not yet out · Sizes or variants: 5M, 28M, 123M, 209M · Released: Hugging Face repositories created 14 October 2026; Mistral blog 15 October 2026. Source: https://mistral.ai/news/timestral/
  Model page: https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Timestral-209M

- Black Forest Labs opens FLUX 3 Image Klein, a 5.6B-parameter image generation and editing model, with INT8 and NVFP4 builds for consumer GPUs — Text-to-image, editing and multi-reference generation with up to 10 reference images, native 4K output and bounding-box control of placement or edit regions. BFL's internal evaluations rank it first among open models in text-to-image, multi-reference and editing, and above the closed Ideogram V4.5 and FLUX.2 [pro], with +91 Elo in text-to-image over FLUX.2 [klein] 9B at 38% fewer parameters; these are the lab's own ratings. The generator weights need 11.2 GB in BF16 and 5.6 GB in FP8; NVIDIA-measured times are about 4 seconds per 1 MP image on an RTX 5090 and under 9 seconds on an RTX 4070 (INT8, distilled model, 16 steps), and the INT8 build runs on 8 GB of VRAM. This is the small open sibling of FLUX 3 Image (API, covered 2 October). Artefacts: weights (FLUX Kommunity License, gated on Hugging Face), base and step-distilled models, FP8/INT8/NVFP4 builds, BFL API, Diffusers support · Sizes or variants: 5.6B generator (text encoders not counted) · Released: 15 October 2026 (Hugging Face repositories created 13 to 14 October). Source: https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-image-klein
  Model page: https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/flux-3-image-klein
