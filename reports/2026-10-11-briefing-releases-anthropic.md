## 1. United States

- H2O-Lightning-31B (H2O.ai) — A 31B typed "decision model" fine-tuned from Gemma 4 31B-it, published on Hugging Face late on 10 October. Like the Strands and Liquid AI d1 models already covered, it answers choice, yes/no and ordinal-score questions about a record (text plus up to four images) in one forward pass and returns calibrated probabilities. H2O's own numbers: 212/231 (91.8 %) on JevBench's public items in FP8 on one H100 at about 25 ms p50, and 88.4 % over 1,877 questions on 16 public image sets against 88.0 % for the frozen Gemma base (a +0.4 point gain whose 90 % interval, -0.3 to +1.0, includes zero). The card admits that long records are cut to 3,000 tokens by a retrieval shim, that serving is validated only with prefix caching off, and that smaller GPUs are unmeasured. It runs on unmodified vLLM 0.30.0 behind a small shim (`/v1/systemone`), and the NOTICE file points out that Gemma's prohibited-use policy bars automated decisions that affect individual rights. The same day H2O pushed v1.2.3 of its 4B sibling (H2O-Lightning-4B, Qwen3.5-4B base, first uploaded 2 October) with re-measured latency (about 29 ms) and a GGUF/llama.cpp package, after correcting latency figures that Microsoft's Decision-1 announcement had used in its comparison.
