- Stop building software factories — Justin McCarthy, founder and CEO of Diffusion, at AI Engineer. Counter-position to the "software factory" talks of the past week: when you do not yet know what you want, pushing new ideas through an agent factory wastes time and tokens. He argues for "brownfield" factories, continuous 24/7 loops that keep the promises of software customers already pay for and get faster, cheaper and safer, and says they simplify with every model release and take about a week to stand up (his claim, no measurement given in the description). His closing point is economic: with code abundant, the bottleneck is demand, so engineers should build a "demand factory" that finds what customers will buy.
