## Builders and founders

- Noam Brown (OpenAI researcher), live at Sequoia's No Limit pokerbot finals: why game theory did not lead to general intelligence — Brown, who built the first AIs to beat top poker professionals, describes a "crisis of faith": game theory assumes rational opponents, and the Diplomacy bots that won were those trained on human data to model real behaviour, which led him to the reasoning work behind o1. He says he asks every new model to build a pokerbot, that GPT-6 Astra still falls short there, that this is the "centaur period" of human plus AI, and, as his own "most controversial take", that promising students should drop out and join the labs. First-person account from an OpenAI employee; the claims are his. Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tRaFbDFBl_A
