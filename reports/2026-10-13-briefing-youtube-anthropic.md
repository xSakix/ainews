- Decoupling the Brain from the Hands: Claude Managed Agents (Part 1 of 4) — Priyanka Phatak, who leads the Claude Managed Agents engineering team at Anthropic, defines an agent as a model in a loop with tools plus a harness and a sandbox, and argues that harnesses encode assumptions that go stale with each new model. She walks through the first design (session, harness and sandbox in one hard-to-debug "pet" container) and the redesign into an append-only session log, a "brain" (harness plus Claude) and "hands" (sandboxes and tools). Credentials are injected at an egress proxy so Claude never sees them, and time to first token dropped by about 90 percent (the speaker's figure). Part of an AI Engineer workshop series (parts 2-4 cover a research agent, scheduled agents and "dreaming", and evals/CI/multi-tenancy). - Your GPU Neighbor Can Hear Your Model — Branislav Brzak (Stealthium) demonstrates an H100 timing side channel: a tenant probing only its own memory sees about three microseconds of extra latency when another tenant's GPU slice is active, without exploiting a software bug. He states explicitly that prompts and answers are not recovered and that results hold for the tested setups. Relevant to anyone sharing GPUs between tenants.
