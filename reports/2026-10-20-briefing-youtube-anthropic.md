## AI engineering and research

- Subbarao Kambhampati: "On the Mythos of LLM 'Thinking Traces'" — A research talk (Tübingen ELLIS/MPI, 9 October) on the claims that grew up around reasoning models after DeepSeek R1: that the intermediate tokens are an interpretable window into the model's "thinking", that their length tracks task difficulty, and that RL post-training works by lengthening them. Kambhampati's group says its experiments dissect each claim with "counterintuitive conclusions", and he discusses what this means for agentic systems and for getting verifiable problem-solving traces in safety-critical use. Worth the reader's time for the cognitive-side question of what a reasoning trace actually is. The speaker's thesis, not an established result.
