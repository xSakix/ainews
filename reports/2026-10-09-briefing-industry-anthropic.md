## 2. Policy and regulation

- **Anthropic publishes its 2026 Usage Policy, effective 12 November: new deceptive-campaigns section, weapons-software and surveillance wording, and a ban on persistent abuse of the model** — Changes: election rules narrowed to deceiving voters or disrupting elections ("Do Not Undermine Democratic Processes", with the blanket ban on personalised vote targeting removed); a consolidated ban on fake-account and influence-operation tooling; weapons rules extended to guidance and control software and to arming drones; a rule that Claude cannot decide or recommend who to investigate, arrest or charge; new controls for autonomous physical actions; and clearer high-risk health and finance requirements.
