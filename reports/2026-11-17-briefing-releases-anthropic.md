## 3. China

The multimodal flagship takes text, image and video, thinks by default with an adjustable reasoning effort, supports parallel tool calling, web search and JSON Schema output, and is compatible with the OpenAI Chat Completions and Responses APIs and Anthropic's Messages API. The preview documentation gives a 1M-token context and 393,216 maximum output tokens, shared between thinking and answer; the production page's spec table was not read in full, so those two figures are the preview's. International pricing per Alibaba's pricing page: $1.5 per million input and $4.5 per million output tokens (up to 1M tokens), against $2 and $6 for qwen3.8-max and $2.5 and $7.5 for qwen3.7-max, i.e. a flagship tier that gets cheaper with each generation; cache reads are $0.075 per million. The preview was meant to retire on 25 November. Benchmarks are Alibaba's own and none were checked; the open-weights release promised for Qwen 4 (a December date was reported by third-party trackers, not read on a primary page) is not part of this release. What is new: general availability, pricing and the end of the Token-Plan-only preview. ## 5. Tools, APIs and platform updates

- Hugging Face switches Inference Providers to OpenRouter's router — Hugging Face now routes language-model requests made with a Hugging Face token through OpenRouter, which picks a provider per request by live price, speed and reliability and retries on another provider on failure. It supports OpenAI Chat Completions and Responses and Anthropic Messages for every chat model; pricing is unchanged with no markup, and a breaking change affects custom provider keys. The post cites OpenRouter data: open-weight models rose from 26.8% of tokens served in October 2025 to 71.7% at the end of September 2026 (peak 77.5% in July); open-weight models average $0.28 per million input and $1.62 output versus $1.27 and $6.36 for closed models; on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index the best open-weight model (Kimi K3.1 Pro) scores 46 versus 65 for Claude Fable 5.2, at about $3.68 versus $26.54 per task. OpenRouter's own measurements of its new routing: 46% fewer capacity errors, 16.9% better time to first token, 16.3% better throughput. Those are vendor figures. Artefacts: API/platform change · Released: 16 November 2026. Source: https://huggingface.co/blog/openrouter
