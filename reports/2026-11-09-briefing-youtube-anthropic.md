## 1. AI engineering and research

- What the Top Companies Do Differently With AI Coding — Martin Harrysson and Prakhar Dixit (McKinsey) report what they see across large companies using agents in software development: most still see modest gains, but roughly the top 20 to 25% report 2x end-to-end improvements. The patterns they name are shorter agent-driven cycles instead of two-week sprints, smaller pods with merged roles, curated skills and shared context, and change management beyond pilots; they also say quality can drop as code generation speeds up and the bottleneck moves to requirements and release, and that measuring impact is still the hardest part. The figures are McKinsey's own observations, not a published study with a method, so treat them as a consultancy's claim; the talk is useful for the economy-and-work thread on how teams reorganise around agents.
